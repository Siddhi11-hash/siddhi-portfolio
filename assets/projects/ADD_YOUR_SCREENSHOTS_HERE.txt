Drop your project screenshots in this folder using these exact filenames
so they automatically appear (with a 3D flip-reveal animation) on the
matching project card. No code changes needed — just add the files:

  neuroweb.png         -> NeuroWeb (featured project, index.html)
  pinit.png            -> PinIt (index.html)
  voting-system.png    -> Online Voting System (index.html)
  doc-converter.png    -> Multi-Format Document Converter (more-projects.html)

Tips:
- Recommended size: ~1200x750px (16:10), JPG or PNG, ideally under 500KB.
- A browser screenshot of your live app/demo works best.
- Each card shows a themed icon "poster" (brain for NeuroWeb, cap for
  PinIt, ballot for Voting, export icon for Doc Converter) on the front.
  Hovering flips the card over to reveal your screenshot on the back.
- Until you add an image, the back of the flip just shows an "Add
  screenshot" hint instead of a broken image — so the site never looks
  broken even before you've added anything.
- Want more project screens? Copy one of the existing
  <div class="flip-scene">...</div> blocks (from index.html or
  more-projects.html), give its front face a new icon/label, and point
  its <img src="..."> at a new filename you drop in this folder.
